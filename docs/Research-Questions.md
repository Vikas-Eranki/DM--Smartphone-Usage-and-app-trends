# **Research Questions**

---

### **Research Paper Reference**

[IEEE Research Paper – “Understanding App Usage Behavior Through Multi-Contextual Data Mining”](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?arnumber=9745583)

---

## **Overview**

This document outlines the structured research questions derived from the referenced IEEE paper.  
The questions are categorized into **four primary domains** — _App Domain_, _User Domain_, _Smartphone Domain_, and _Cross-Domain (Ethics & Privacy)_ — to facilitate focused exploration within each research area.

---

## **A. App Domain**

### **1. Impact of App Attributes on Ratings and Installs**

How do app characteristics (such as category, size, price, and content rating) influence user ratings and the number of installs across different app genres?

### **2. Feature-Rich vs. Lightweight Apps: Trade-offs & Retention**

How do feature-rich apps (large size, multiple permissions, many functionalities) compare to lightweight apps in terms of user satisfaction, update frequency, and long-term retention across different categories?

## **B. Smartphone and User Efficiency Domain**

### **1. Behavioral Modeling of Smartphone Efficiency**

How do screen time, number of apps installed, and category-wise app usage collectively affect smartphone usage efficiency, and can we model the behavioral patterns that define an ‘efficient’ vs ‘inefficient’ smartphone user?

### **2. Digital Wellbeing & Overuse Detection**

How can patterns of daily screen time combined with category-wise app usage be used to identify early signs of smartphone overuse or digital burnout among different demographic groups?

---

## **C. Cross-Domain (Ethics & Privacy)**

### **1. Ethical AI and Data Governance**

What frameworks can ensure ethical data collection and anonymization while maintaining analytical value for large-scale app usage studies?

### **2. Data Privacy & Federated Learning**

Can federated learning frameworks ensure privacy-preserving app usage analysis without compromising accuracy and scalability?

---

## **Summary**

These research questions aim to build a multi-faceted understanding of mobile app usage through:

- **Data-driven insights** into behavioral, contextual, and operational patterns.
- **Ethical and privacy-aware frameworks** for responsible AI-driven analytics.
- **Scalable models** that balance accuracy, transparency, and sustainability.
